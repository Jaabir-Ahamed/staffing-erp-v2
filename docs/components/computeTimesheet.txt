computeTimesheet / promoteNormalizedTimesheet

Pure calculation engine (no UI). computeTimesheet derives bonus, absence penalty,
gross, net (floor 0), client charge, profit, and needs_attention from the current
row plus payroll_rule_sets / client_rate_rules. Recalculating with a fix (pay rate,
trade, worker match) auto-clears a resolved flag and may replace it with the next
remaining reason.

promoteNormalizedTimesheet will not persist a timesheet without a matched
worker_id and billing client_id. Status cannot leave draft while needs_attention
is true (DB trigger + canLeaveDraft).
